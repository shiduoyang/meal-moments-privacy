Meal Moments Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-18

Meal Moments is an offline food diary that requires no account. Your diary stays on this device and is not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Information processed

The app processes the following information only when you use the related feature:

  • Meal text, time, and notes, to create, search, and review entries.
  • Photos that you choose to attach to an entry.
  • Location, read once after you tap Add location, to create an editable location label.
  • Microphone audio while voice input is active, to convert speech into text. Audio is not saved.

Storage and sharing

Entries, photos, and settings are stored in the app's local storage. The app has no Internet permission, creates no account, uploads nothing to a developer server, and does not sell or share personal information.

You control where an exported backup is saved or shared. An unencrypted backup can be read directly. Backup passwords are not retained by the app.

Permissions and components

Camera, photo, microphone, and location permissions are requested only when the corresponding feature is used. Refusing an optional permission does not prevent text-only diary use.

The app uses Capacitor for native capabilities, SQLite for on-device records, and sherpa-ncnn for offline speech recognition. These components do not transmit diary content through this app.

Your choices and rights

You can view, edit, or delete entries in the app and export all data from Data & Backup. Uninstalling removes local app data, so export a backup first if needed.

You can withdraw privacy consent from Data & Backup. Withdrawal does not automatically delete existing data, but the app returns to the privacy consent screen until you consent again.
